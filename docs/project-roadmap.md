# Executive Summary

This portfolio reflects a business-first approach to data. It combines technical implementation with clear decision support and measurable business outcomes.

The three projects together show a progression from:
- historical reporting,
- operational automation,
- to future-focused prediction.

That progression is valuable because it illustrates a mature understanding of how data is used inside organizations to support action, reduce risk, and improve performance.

## Portfolio Narrative

### Descriptive Analytics
The first project establishes the ability to interpret historical business data and present it clearly to executives and decision-makers.

### Operational Automation
The second project shows the ability to automate the flow of information and build repeatable systems that improve data freshness and reduce manual effort.

### Predictive Modeling
The third project demonstrates the ability to move beyond retrospective analysis and apply forecasting to protect revenue and improve customer retention.

This combination is especially compelling for employers and clients seeking a practical, results-oriented data professional.
