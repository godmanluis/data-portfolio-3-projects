# Executive Summary

This portfolio reflects a business-first approach to analytical work. It combines technical execution with practical decision support and measurable business impact.

The three projects in this repository tell a cohesive story:

- descriptive analytics to understand what happened
- automation to improve how data flows through an organization
- predictive modeling to identify what may happen next

That progression is especially valuable in business environments where data is expected to support action, not simply provide reporting output.

Across these projects, I demonstrate the ability to:
- translate business questions into analytical tasks
- clean and structure data for decision-making
- automate recurring data work
- build models that support business strategy
- communicate findings in a business-ready format
